      Weather along your route

      Your weather companion

      Route Weather by Andy's Dev Studio combines route forecasts, NOAA radar, and Caltrans feeds used by QuickMap. Address search and routing are powered by TomTom. Live traffic availability depends on service capacity.

      Map libraries: Leaflet (license), Leaflet Rotate (GPLv3), and Mapbox vector-tile, pbf and point-geometry. Copyright and license notices are included with this app.

      Route Weather 0.32 (v0.21 GPLv3 source code, v0.21 source archive) · NWS route forecasts, NOAA HRRR precipitation via Iowa State IEM, Caltrans road layers, NIFC fire and satellite feeds, and state road links from the FHWA directory.

      Planning: add a destination, optional waypoints, departure, and total stop time. Forecast points use estimated arrival times. Stop time is spread proportionally along the route. Routing uses TomTom's standard driving profile and does not check truck restrictions or road closures.

      GPS: follow shows your live location. Turn on Voice below Layers to hear directions along the planned route while the app is open. Use a truck navigation app for truck restrictions.

      Conditions: wind, gusts, visibility and precipitation are model forecasts. Icing labels are temperature/precipitation estimates, not pavement measurements. Radar shows recent observed precipitation, not a forecast. Roads are filtered by proximity, so nearby roads can appear too.

      Privacy: your trip is stored on this browser. Coordinates and address searches are sent to the data providers to retrieve results. Location is requested on startup to center your blue dot, and when you use GPS features. Privacy policy.

      Sources: Official National Weather Service forecast grids, NOAA/NWS, Caltrans CWWP2, OpenStreetMap, and TomTom for address search and routing. Availability depends on those free services. Maps and live updates require internet; the last trip forecast is saved locally.
