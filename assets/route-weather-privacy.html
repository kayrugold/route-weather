Route Weather Privacy Policy

November 23, 2026 · This policy applies to Route Weather for Android and its browser demo. Andy’s Dev Studio operates both versions. Route Weather does not require a studio account.

Location and trip information

Route Weather asks for location access on startup to center the map on your blue location dot, and when you choose a GPS feature. It uses your location, speed and heading to follow your drive and provide directions. You can deny or revoke location permission and enter a start and destination manually. Address and business queries, the search area when finding nearby places, and route coordinates go to the Route Weather service and TomTom to find places and draw routes. Weather and road features may send coordinates or a map area to the relevant providers, including NOAA and the National Weather Service, Iowa State University’s Iowa Environmental Mesonet, transportation agencies, and wildfire services. Route elevation requests send terrain tile coordinates to the terrain data host. Map and traffic tile requests reveal the area being viewed to the respective map or traffic provider.

Shared road reports

When you submit an obstruction report, Route Weather sends its type and GPS location to the Route Weather service. Other users can see the report’s location, type, and time. Road Ahead sends a nearby search location to look up shared reports. A random identifier saved on your device is sent with submissions and confirmations. The service stores a hashed version to count one vote per installation and let reporters clear their own reports. Reports and votes expire after 90 minutes or become hidden earlier when cleared. Offline submissions are skipped.

Information stored on your device

Route Weather saves trip details, recent destinations, downloaded map data, and preferences locally so you can reuse them. A personal TomTom traffic key, if you choose to enter one, is stored locally and sent to TomTom when that option is used. It is not required for the app’s shared traffic service. Android voice guidance uses an installed offline English speech voice. For voice destination search, your Android or browser speech recognition provider may process your audio. Route Weather receives the recognized text and uses it for search. Browser spoken directions use the browser’s configured speech service.

Use About & sources → Clear saved trip to clear the saved trip. You can remove a personal key in Traffic setup. To remove all app data, including recent destinations, downloaded map data, and preferences, clear Route Weather’s storage in Android settings or clear site data for the browser demo.

Service operation and outside links

The app uses HTTPS for its service requests. Cloudflare hosts the Route Weather service and may process network and request information to operate the service and enforce limits. The service stores aggregate monthly request counts for quota management and does not maintain a server-side trip history. Providers process requests under their own privacy terms. Scanner and radio links open third-party services, which may have their own privacy practices. If you contact Andy’s Dev Studio, we use the information you send to respond and handle your request.
