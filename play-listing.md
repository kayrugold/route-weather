## Short description

Plan a drive around weather, road conditions, and live traffic.

## Full description

Try Route Weather in your browser before buying: https://route-weather.andy-596.workers.dev/

See what the weather is likely to be when you reach each part of your drive. Enter a start and destination, set your departure time, and Route Weather shows forecasts along the route at your estimated arrival times.

• See precipitation, wind, visibility and temperature along your drive.  
• Follow your GPS and turn on spoken directions when you want them.  
• Check radar, weather forecasts and available state road reports.  
• Toggle live traffic to see current speeds where coverage is available.

Plan a trip by entering an address or town, using GPS for your starting point, or dropping a map pin. If you’re looking for a particular restaurant or business, look up the address first and enter it in the planner.

Route Weather uses standard driving routes. It does not check truck height, weight or access restrictions, and spoken directions do not automatically reroute you. Forecasts and road information depend on coverage, your connection and provider availability. Live traffic has usage limits. If traffic is temporarily unavailable, you can still plan routes and check forecasts.

Built by Andy’s Dev Studio. I’d like to hear how you use Route Weather on the road, what you found confusing, and what would make it more useful. Send feedback to andys.dev.studio@gmail.com.
